2. Who sees your information

  • Us. Only the people who run Toast Express can see your name, hall, phone number and orders.
  • The public. If you post a review, your first name, icon, star rating, comment and the date are shown on our website. Your phone number and hall are never shown.
  • Service providers who help us run the site. Our database and login provider (Supabase) stores your account and order data. The company that hosts the website files may also see technical data such as your IP address when you visit. These providers only handle data on our behalf.
  • Our bank sees the transfer you make to us, as part of normal banking.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires us to share information.

We never sell your data or share it for advertising.

3. Transfers outside Nigeria

Supabase stores data on servers outside Nigeria (region chosen by us: ). By creating an account you agree to your data being stored there. We choose providers that use encryption and access controls, and we limit access to the people who need it.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account details while your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we do so within 30 days. We may keep basic order and payment records for longer only where the law or our accounting requires it. Reviews you posted are removed when you ask.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to: show you the data we hold about you; correct it; delete it; limit how we use it; give you a copy in a usable format; or stop using it for something you did not need to agree to. You can withdraw your consent at any time (for example, ask us to remove your review). Contact us using the details above and we will respond within 30 days. You can change your name, hall and icon yourself on your profile.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

6. Security

Connections to the site are encrypted. Passwords are hashed. Each customer can only see their own orders, and only our admin account can see everyone's. If a breach affecting your data ever happens, we will tell the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours, and tell you where the law requires.

7. Children

Toast Express accounts are for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from under-18s. If you think a child has made an account, tell us and we will delete it.

8. Cookies

We use only the browser storage needed to keep you logged in. See our Cookies & storage page.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the version date above. For important changes we will tell you on the site.
