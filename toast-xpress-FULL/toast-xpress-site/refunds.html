In short: if you paid and we could not give you what you ordered, you get your money back. Nothing here removes the rights you have under Nigerian consumer law.

1. We refund in full when

  • you paid, but we cannot make or deliver your order (sold out, closed, a problem in our kitchen, or we cancel it);
  • you paid twice, or paid more than the total (we refund the extra);
  • your order arrives wrong, incomplete, not as described, or there is a problem with its quality or safety.

For the last point, please tell us within hours of delivery, because food is perishable. Send your order code and, if you can, a photo. If you prefer, we can make you a replacement instead of a refund. The choice is yours.

2. Cancelling an order

  • Before you pay: cancel any time with the Cancel button on your order. Nothing is charged.
  • After you pay, before we start making it: contact us straight away and we will refund you in full.
  • After we have started making it: toasts are made fresh to order and cannot be resold, so we cannot refund a change of mind, except in the cases listed in section 1.

3. How you get your money

We refund by bank transfer to an account in your name that you give us, within working days after we agree the refund. We pay the transfer fee. You get back the full amount you were due.

4. How to ask

Call or WhatsApp or email , with your order code and a short description. We will reply as soon as we can and explain our decision. If we cannot agree, you may take your complaint to the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC).
