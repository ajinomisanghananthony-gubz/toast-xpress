Cookies & storage

Version .

In short: we only store what is needed to keep you logged in. No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no social media pixels. That is why there is no cookie pop-up on this site.

What is stored in your browser

WhatWhyHow longNeeds consent?
A login token, kept in your browser's local storage by our login provider (Supabase)Keeps you logged in and proves it is you when you place or view orders.Until you log out or it expiresNo. It is strictly necessary for the service you asked for.

What we do not use

  • No analytics or visitor-tracking tools.
  • No advertising or social media (Facebook, TikTok, Instagram) pixels.
  • No third-party fonts, maps or embedded videos. Our fonts and hero video are served from this website itself.

Other companies you may connect with

  • When you log in or order, your browser talks to our database provider, Supabase, and loads a code file from a public code-delivery network (jsDelivr). They can see your IP address as part of normal internet traffic.
  • If you tap the WhatsApp link, you leave this site and WhatsApp's own terms apply.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics or advertising tools, we will first add a clear banner that lets you accept or reject them, and update this page.

You can clear stored data at any time in your browser settings (you will be logged out). Questions: see our Privacy Policy.
